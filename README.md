# Akintoye Ayomide Nelson

**Senior Backend & Full-Stack Engineer** · Lagos, Nigeria
TypeScript · NestJS · Node.js · Laravel · PostgreSQL · Redis · Docker

[Portfolio](https://akintoyenelson.com/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akintoye-ayomide-nelson) · [X](https://twitter.com/_NelsonAyo) · [Email](mailto:akintoyenelson@gmail.com) · [WhatsApp](https://wa.link/ehqyu9)

I build and run backend systems for payments, marketplaces and real-time products. Over seven years I have shipped event-driven services in NestJS and TypeScript, tuned PostgreSQL and Redis for concurrent load, built payment and webhook flows, and operated the infrastructure underneath them. I also lead full-stack builds with Laravel, Vue and Next.js. I am the founder of [SmartHub Innovation Limited](https://smarthubinnovation.com).

---

## What I work on

- **Payments and fintech:** wallets, bill payments, gift cards, QR payments, and webhook-driven settlement with reconciliation.
- **Real-time, high-concurrency backends:** tournaments, leaderboards, job queues, rate limiting, and fraud and cheat detection.
- **Marketplaces and SaaS:** multi-tenant platforms, software licensing, and metered APIs.
- **Infrastructure and security:** Docker Compose, Nginx and Caddy, hardened Linux servers, backups, observability, and incident recovery.
